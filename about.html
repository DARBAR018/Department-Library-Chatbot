About Us

Inspiring Learning,
Fostering Engineering Excellence

The Department Library is the core knowledge center for Diploma Engineering students and faculty. Established to support advanced academic study, we offer physical catalogs, specialized reference sections, past exam papers, and online access to digital study sheets.

Our facility features modern study cabins, digital terminals, and holds over 5,000 engineering textbook volumes curated specifically for board exam curriculums.
